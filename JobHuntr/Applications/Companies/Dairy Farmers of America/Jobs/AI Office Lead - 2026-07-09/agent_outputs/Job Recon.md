## Culture Signals

DFA is a **farmer-owned global dairy cooperative** (~9,000 farmer-owners, ~19,000 employees, $24B+ revenue) headquartered in Kansas City, KS. Culture emphasizes:

- **Mission-driven:** "Deliver value to family farm-owners" — purpose beyond profit
- **Collaboration & belonging:** Cooperative structure; ERGs (GroPro, EmpowHER); inclusion as strategic enabler
- **Integrity & compliance:** Code of Conduct; governance and ethical business conduct are explicit values
- **Innovation with pragmatism:** DFA CoLAB Accelerator (since 2017) mentors ag-tech startups including AI (CattleEye, Metha.ai, Bankbarn); innovation is valued but grounded in dairy operations
- **Sustainability & community:** Recent $46M USDA AMP grant (Feb 2026) for conservation and small-farm market access signals long-horizon, stakeholder-aware strategy
- **Stability over startup chaos:** Employee testimonials emphasize job security, supportive teams, competitive pay/vacation — more enterprise cooperative than VC-backed tech

**JD tone:** Formal corporate HR language; heavy emphasis on governance, compliance, executive communication, and measurable business value — not a "move fast break things" startup posting.
