## About

Dairy Farmers of America (DFA) is a farmer-owned dairy cooperative. Members focus on high-quality milk production; DFA handles marketing, manufacturing, and distribution for popular dairy brands. The cooperative emphasizes nutrition, community, innovation, and sustainability across its operations.
