Spur Reply, part of the Reply Network, is a leader in go-to-market consulting. Our mission is to turn every interaction our clients have into a go-to-market advantage. Our objective is to drive results that matter. As the leading authority on go-to-market solutions, we act as an extension of our client teams, providing the support, tools, and strategies to achieve important outcomes. If you tackle challenges with unmatched persistence and believe learning is critical to ongoing growth, we want you on our team.
As a Marketing Project Manager in a temp role, you will support managing multiple key workstreams and initiatives on behalf of our client. You will help make impact by driving clarity, organization, and follow-through across timelines, stakeholders, and deliverables. This role focuses on keeping work moving, maintaining trackers and workback plans, and ensuring teams have what they need to ship on time.
This temporary position requires prior experience either working in a consulting capacity with Microsoft or having direct experience at Microsoft.
Responsibilities
Own day-to-day project management and workback planning across all client workstreams by tracking what is shipping, when, who owns it, and what is blocked
Create communications for team BOM as needed, including call for content, pre-reads, recaps, agendas, and execution plans
Maintain and build all team workstream calendars, operational frameworks, workback plans, and execution plans, including SharePoint list tracking system
Drive needed rhythms to document and track any shifting timelines, stakeholders, or requirements and drive needed client stakeholder alignment to track task completion, flag changes/risk, and ensure deliverables are ready when needed
Develop workbacks, assets, and communications to track budget spend
Maintain comprehensive checklist and workback plan with milestone tracking, progress updates, and action items for all key moment/events
Produce and update process documentation frameworks and guidelines quarterly
Maintain a Teams/SharePoint repository for all assets and documentation
Minimum Requirements
A Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Business Administration, Communications, or related degree
3 years of experience in project management, program management, business management, marketing operations, and data analysis
3 years of professional experience required (consulting experience a plus)
Preference for client-facing professional services experience
Advanced experience with Microsoft Suite of productivity tools (Word, PowerPoint, Excel, Outlook), and SharePoint Lists
Power BI, Tableau, HubSpot, QuickBase, SharePoint, OneNote experience is a plus
