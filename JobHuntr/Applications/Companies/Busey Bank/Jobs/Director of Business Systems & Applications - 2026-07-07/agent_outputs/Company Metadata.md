# Busey Bank — Company Profile

**Source:** https://www.busey.com (fetched 2026-07-07)

---

## Overview

Busey Bank is a community-oriented FDIC-insured financial institution headquartered in Champaign, Illinois. The bank emphasizes customer-focused solutions and active community engagement.

---

## Business Focus

- **Personal & Commercial Banking** — full-service retail and business accounts
- **Mortgage Lending** — residential and commercial mortgages
- **Wealth Management** — investment and portfolio services
- **Agricultural Services** — farm and agricultural lending
- **Small Business Administration (SBA) Lending**
- **Digital Banking** — Busey Digital Banking platform with mobile app

---

## Organizational Characteristics

- **Philosophy:** "The Busey Way" — customer and community-focused
- **Community Involvement:** Active in school programs, financial education, scholarships, community shred days
- **Geographic Presence:** Based in Champaign, IL; multiple branch locations across Kansas and Texas (per job posting: Leawood KS, Dallas TX, Fort Worth TX)

---

## Industry Context

- Regulated financial services (FDIC insurance)
- Competitive landscape: Regional community bank competing with larger national banks and fintech solutions
- Regulatory environment: Banking compliance, consumer protection, reserve requirements, anti-money laundering
