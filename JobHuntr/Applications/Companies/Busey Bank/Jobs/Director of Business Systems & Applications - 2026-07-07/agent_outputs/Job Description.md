## Position Summary

A strategic leadership role overseeing the performance and evolution of Busey's applications and systems. The director ensures alignment with business objectives while managing core banking systems, digital platforms, and integrated third-party solutions. This position emphasizes collaboration with senior stakeholders to implement cost-effective technology strategies that support growth and regulatory compliance.

---

## Key Responsibilities

- Lead and mentor the Business Systems and Applications team
- Develop application and integration strategy supporting digital transformation
- Identify and implement cost-effective technology solutions with executive leadership
- Ensure performance, resilience, and optimization of core processing systems
- Manage relationships with key strategic vendors and service delivery
- Lead vendor selection and onboarding for new applications
- Develop KPIs to measure application effectiveness
- Ensure regulatory compliance; partner with Audit, Risk, and Compliance teams
- Implement best practices for incident management and change control
- Monitor fintech trends and regulatory standards
- Communicate effectively with technical and non-technical audiences
