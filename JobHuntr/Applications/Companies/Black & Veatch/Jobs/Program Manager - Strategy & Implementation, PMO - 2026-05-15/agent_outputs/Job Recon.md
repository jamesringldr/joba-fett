## Role Snapshot

Senior staff-level Program Manager inside Black & Veatch's Infrastructure Advisory "Integrated PMO" team, leading the Strategy & Implementation function for B&V's emerging **PMO-as-a-Service (PMOaaS)** offering. The role owns the proposal funnel, cross-functional resource planning, KPI/operational analytics, an internal "centralized warehouse" of PMO IP, training curriculum build-out, and an AI/automation roadmap for PMO operations. It is part operations leader, part internal product/program owner, and part strategic advisor to PMO leadership.

2. Proposal / bid funnel management — risk, financial health, growth-opportunity assessment
3. Cross-functional resource demand planning, capacity modeling, staffing pipeline
4. KPI definition, tracking, dashboarding, and operational analytics ("voice of Operations")
5. Process improvement / continuous improvement / operational optimization in complex matrixed orgs
6. Stakeholder communication and influence with senior leadership in a matrixed environment
7. Strong financial management and client-facing skills
8. Strategic advisory — pipeline health, account forecasting, resource allocation, GTM planning
9. Building internal knowledge/IP repositories ("centralized warehouse" from ground up)

12. Waterfall, Agile, and hybrid delivery lifecycles

- **100% employee-owned (ESOP)** — pitch in language of ownership, long-term thinking, shared success.

## Key Themes (what they are really hiring for)

1. **Stand up PMOaaS as a productized offering** — proposal engine, pricing, delivery playbooks, and the "warehouse" of reusable assets.
2. **Operationalize a matrixed delivery network** — make resource demand and staffing transparent across sectors so PMs can scale without bottlenecks.
3. **Turn PMO into a data-driven function** — KPIs, dashboards, forecasting accuracy, pipeline health visible to senior leadership.
4. **Bring AI/automation into PMO operations** — evaluate, pilot, and integrate AI platforms and system integrators to drive scalability.
5. **Build the bench** — design and launch the training curriculum so any B&V professional can step in and run a PMOaaS engagement.

## Red Flags / Watch-Outs

- **Travel up to 100%** with **no relocation and no visa sponsorship** — candidate must be Overland Park-anchored and road-ready.
- Hybrid scope (proposal lead + resource planner + KPI owner + curriculum builder + AI roadmap) is broad; risk of unclear charter or ownership conflict with existing PMO leadership. Probe for headcount, authority, and reporting line in interviews.
- "Management Responsibilities" section is empty in the JD — likely an individual contributor / player-coach role despite the leadership scope. Worth confirming.
- B&V Glassdoor signal flags WFH policy changes and decisions perceived as top-down; an operational leader here will need diplomatic communication skills.
- "PMOaaS" appears to still be maturing as an offering — exciting build opportunity, but expect ambiguity and shifting priorities.
