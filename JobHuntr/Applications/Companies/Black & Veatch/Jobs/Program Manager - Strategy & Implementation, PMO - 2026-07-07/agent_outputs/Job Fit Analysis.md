### 4. Strategic Value Assessment

**Why this role is worth pursuing:**

1. **Escape Velocity from Healthcare IT:** You've built significant expertise in healthcare; this role lets you apply your PMO mastery to a different domain (infrastructure). Diversifying your sector expertise increases long-term optionality.

2. **Organizational Leadership:** This isn't a player-coach role; it's clearly strategic leadership. You'd be designing PMO practices across an organization with 7,000+ active projects. This is a significant scope increase.

3. **100% Employee-Owned Culture:** Black & Veatch's ownership model attracts long-term thinkers. If you're considering a return to full-time employment, this culture aligns with your ethos better than many public companies.

4. **Local Anchor:** Overland Park is your home. Minimal commute, existing network, family stability.

5. **Startup Optionality:** If Vanyshr progresses, you could potentially negotiate consulting or part-time arrangements post-acquisition or funding. B&V's size and structure sometimes allows for strategic side projects for proven leaders.

**Why it might not be the right move:**

1. **Startup Conflict:** Vanyshr is actively being built. A 100% travel role would require stepping back significantly.

2. **Founder Identity:** You've been building your own thing for 2+ years. Re-entering a large organization (even with ownership culture) is a psychological shift.

3. **Role Scope Creep:** "PMO as a Service" strategy across a 13,000-person organization is complex. This could be a grind if the organization isn't aligned.
