## Red Flags / Watch-Outs

- **100% Travel Expectation:** "Up to 100% travel" is significant. Clarify in interviews: is this typical, or are there cycles? Does remote strategic work count as travel mitigation?

- **PMO Role Ambiguity:** Is this role primarily operational PMO (KPI tracking, reporting) or strategic PMO (advisory, transformation)? JD hints both; clarify expectations.

- **Organization Dynamics:** In a 13,000-person organization, PMO success depends heavily on executive sponsorship. Ask about current PMO perception, maturity level, and executive buy-in.

- **"AI & Automation" Hype:** Role mentions leveraging AI for automation but doesn't specify current maturity. Is this greenfield or existing tools? What's the budget/authority?

- **Regulatory Complexity:** Infrastructure/utilities = highly regulated. Ensure you're comfortable with compliance-heavy environments if you haven't worked in them.

## Interview Intel

**Questions to Ask:**

1. **PMO Maturity & Scope:**
   - What's the current state of PMO processes? (Ad-hoc vs. standardized vs. optimized)
   - How many people report into PMO? Who are key stakeholders?
   - What's the executive perception of PMO today?

2. **Travel Reality:**
   - Can you break down the 100% travel? (What's typical week/month?)
   - Is there flexibility for strategic/planning work to be remote?

3. **AI/Automation Specifics:**
   - What tools/platforms are you evaluating?
   - What's the budget and timeline?
   - Will I have hiring authority for data/automation specialists?

4. **Success Metrics:**
   - How will my performance be measured? (Delivery %, cost variance, adoption rate, etc.)
   - What would success look like in Year 1?

5. **Culture Fit:**
   - Tell me about employee ownership in practice. How does it shape decision-making?
   - What does "PMO as a Service" mean to you?
