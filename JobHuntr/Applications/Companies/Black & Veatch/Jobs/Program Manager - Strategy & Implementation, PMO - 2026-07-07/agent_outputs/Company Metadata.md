## Company Overview

Black & Veatch is a global engineering, procurement, consulting, and construction (EPC) solutions provider specializing in sustainable infrastructure development.

**Mission:** "Building a World of Difference®"  
**Core Mission:** Create infrastructure that protects the planet and empowers all people.

- **Status:** Privately held
- **Geographic Reach:** Global operations across multiple continents
