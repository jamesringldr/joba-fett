# Senior Strategic Program Manager at Jack Henry

**Company:** Jack Henry
**Industry:** Financial Services
**Location:** Hybrid (2+ days/week in Springfield, MO / Monett, MO / Lenexa, KS / Allen, TX)
**Work Hours:** 8 AM–5 PM CST with flexibility

## Key Responsibilities

The role involves owning "complex, multi-threaded programs from strategy through delivery" while managing vendor relationships and cross-functional collaboration. Primary duties include program lifecycle oversight, stakeholder engagement, risk management, and facilitating alignment across business units.

## Required Qualifications

- 6+ years leading enterprise-scale programs/projects
- Bachelor's degree in Project Management, Business Administration, or related field
- Ability to analyze complex initiatives and translate them into executable projects
- Proficiency creating presentations for senior leadership
- Willingness to travel up to 15%

## Preferred Skills

The posting notes "nice-to-have" credentials including PMP certification, Scrum experience, and cloud technology background. Cloud project management experience and software development project management are listed as additional qualifications.

## Why This Matters

Jack Henry is undertaking a major cloud migration to Google Cloud Platform by 2030, and this position offers "direct impact on and exposure to" that transformation initiative. The company emphasizes being a "Best Place to Work" with strong employee support.
