5. **"Microsoft Azure" spelled out at first use.** Previously just "Azure" — minor ATS robustness improvement.
6. **"Google Cloud Platform (GCP)" spelled out at first use.** Both the full name and the GCP acronym now appear in the summary and Core Competencies so either keyword form matches.
7. **"Chief Financial Officer (CFO)" spelled out.** Acronym expanded at first use.
8. **"Voice of the Customer (VOC)" spelled out at first use.** Acronym best practice.
9. **Contact separators changed from middle-dot (·) to pipe (|).** Some older ATSes mishandle special characters; pipes are universally safe.
10. **Section dividers: dates moved to their own line.** Date ranges are now on a dedicated line in "Month YYYY – Month YYYY" format under each role, which is the format ATSes most reliably parse.
11. **Italics removed from company descriptors.** Replaced markdown italics (`*non-profit...*`) with plain text — eliminates a parse-risk for ATSes that strip formatting unpredictably.
12. **"Project / Program Manager" → "Project and Program Manager."** Slashes can occasionally split into two tokens; "and" parses cleanly.
13. **"Cross-functional alignment" → "Cross-functional collaboration"** in Core Competencies — exact JD phrasing.
14. **"Multi-threaded" + "from strategy through delivery"** now appear verbatim together in the Avirodha lead bullet (mirroring the JD's exact phrasing about owning "complex, multi-threaded programs from strategy through delivery").
15. **Em-dashes preserved but flanked by spaces.** Acceptable for modern ATSes; tested format.
16. **Removed Rewrite Notes section.** Internal scratch — should not appear in the resume document the ATS scans.
17. **No PMP fabricated.** Cert block stays CSM + CSPO. PMP is a JD nice-to-have, not a hard requirement.
